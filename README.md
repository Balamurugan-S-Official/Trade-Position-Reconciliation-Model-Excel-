**#Trade & Position Reconciliation Model (Excel)**

--Developed an Excel-based reconciliation model to compare internal trade ledger records against custodian statements for 18 equity and fixed-income trades, achieving a 77.78% match rate.

--Automated two-way trade matching using VLOOKUP, IFERROR, and IF/AND formulas to identify discrepancies in trade quantity, cash amounts, and settlement status.

--Investigated and documented 4 operational breaks, including a broker fee cash difference, unbooked corporate action bonus shares, a counterparty DK settlement fail, and a missing trade.

--Created a Break Tracker MIS dashboard using COUNTIF and date-based formulas to monitor open breaks, track aging, and assign action owners across Middle Office (MO) and Back Office (BO).

--Improved reconciliation visibility by organizing matched items, exceptions, and follow-up actions in a structured Excel workflow.

Tools & Concepts: Microsoft Excel, VLOOKUP, IFERROR, IF/AND, COUNTIF, Trade Reconciliation, Position Reconciliation, Break Investigation, Settlement Monitoring, Break Aging, Middle Office & Back Office Operations.
